Timeline of Internet Memes

This article details the historical timeline of notable internet memes, viral videos, and internet phenomena with video documentation.

1996

The Dancing Baby (Baby Cha-Cha)

One of the oldest viral internet phenomena. A 3D-rendered animation of a baby dancing to Hooked on a Feeling, rendered as a source file demonstration for 3D animation software.

2001

All Your Base Are Belong To Us

A viral meme derived from the poorly translated opening cutscene of the 1989 Sega Mega Drive version of the video game Zero Wing.

2003

Numa Numa

Gary Brolsma posted a video of himself lip-syncing to the song "Dragostea Din Tei" by O-Zone. It became one of the first major web video viral phenomena.

2007

Rickrolling (Rick Astley - Never Gonna Give You Up)

An internet bait-and-switch meme where a user provides a hyperlink disguised as relevant content, which instead redirects to the 1987 music video by Rick Astley.

2011

Nyan Cat

A 8-bit animation of a cat with a Pop-Tart body flying through space while leaving a rainbow trail behind it, set to a Japanese pop song.

2012

Gangnam Style

South Korean musician Psy released "Gangnam Style", becoming the first YouTube video to reach 1 billion views and generating globally recognizable video dances.
